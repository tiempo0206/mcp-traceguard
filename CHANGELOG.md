## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-19

- Capture deterministic MCP tool catalogs over in-memory, stdio, and
  Streamable HTTP transports.
- Detect policy violations and semantic contract drift with explainable risk
  contributions and SARIF 2.1.0 export.
- Guard tool calls with deny-by-default rules, argument conditions, approval
  gates, recursive redaction, and tamper-evident traces.
- Replay an eight-case adversarial security pack.
- Review five evidence formats in the local TypeScript Evidence Studio.
- Reproduce catalog-scale measurements and enforce portable CI budgets.
- Publish strict JSON Schemas, compatibility evidence, threat model, tutorial,
  architecture, and resume claim boundaries.
